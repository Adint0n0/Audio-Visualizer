The optional AAC encoder bundles an unmodified FFmpeg AAC encoder in WebAssembly.
FFmpeg is copyright the FFmpeg developers. Its LGPL 2.1+ license and upstream
license information accompany this application in licenses/ffmpeg/.
The encoder is loaded as a separate module. Its source, C bridge, worker, and
build recipe are in packages/aac-encoder of the corresponding Mediabunny release.
FFmpeg source: https://ffmpeg.org/download.html
See licenses/ffmpeg/REBUILD.md for the exact FFmpeg revision, checksum,
Emscripten toolchain and commands for rebuilding with modified FFmpeg source.
